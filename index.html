On-Policy Distillation · Rollout Efficiency同策略蒸馏 · Rollout 效率

Flash-OPD

Fast on-policy distillation that stops every rollout at its own reliability boundary.快速同策略蒸馏：
让每条 rollout 都停在自己的可靠性边界上。

Shared rollout horizons cut some trajectories too early and over-serve others. Flash-OPD interleaves cached generation with teacher verification. It retires each trajectory once its exact count of low-compatibility events reaches a budget, which delivers 2.2×–7.5× speedups over full OPD at matched or better accuracy.共享的 rollout horizon 会让一部分轨迹截断过早，又让另一部分被过度解码。Flash-OPD 把带缓存的生成与教师验证交替进行：一旦某条轨迹的低兼容事件精确计数达到预算，就立即让它停止。在准确率持平甚至更高的前提下，相比完整 OPD 实现 2.2×–7.5× 加速。

01 · The problem01 · 问题

One horizon per batch can't fit every trajectory.整个 batch 共用一个 horizon，适配不了每条轨迹。

OPD accelerators like TOPD (fixed truncation) and Prune-OPD (feedback-adapted horizon) choose a single rollout length M per batch. Reliable lengths vary across trajectories, so any M both cuts useful supervision on some rollouts and wastes decoding on others. Drag the horizon and see for yourself.TOPD（固定截断）和 Prune-OPD（按反馈自适应 horizon）这类 OPD 加速方法，都给整个 batch 选同一个 rollout 长度 M。可不同轨迹的可靠长度差别很大：任何一个 M 都会在一些 rollout 上截掉有用的监督，又在另一些上白白浪费解码。拖动下面的 horizon 亲自试试。

Why it happens为什么会这样

Reliability boundaries are heterogeneous within a batch and drift over training. On R1-1.5B ← JustRL-1.5B, TOPD's fixed 4,096 first exceeds the whole reliability range and later falls below its mean. On Qwen3-1.7B-Base, both TOPD and Prune-OPD stay far above Emax. A more sensitive horizon controller can't fix this, because the decision is made at the wrong granularity.可靠性边界在 batch 内高度异质，并且随训练不断漂移。在 R1-1.5B ← JustRL-1.5B 上，TOPD 固定的 4,096 起初超过整个可靠范围，后来又低于其均值；在 Qwen3-1.7B-Base 上，TOPD 和 Prune-OPD 都远高于 Emax。换一个更灵敏的 horizon 控制器也解决不了，因为决策的粒度本身就错了。

02 · The idea02 · 核心思想

Don't predict the boundary. Verify it.不去预测边界，而是验证边界。

Flash-OPD counts top-k disagreements between student and teacher. Reliability is defined as a first-passage time of that count. The boundary is unknown before sampling, but whether a prefix has crossed it can be checked exactly.Flash-OPD 统计学生与教师之间 top-k 不一致的次数，并把可靠性定义为这个计数的首达时间（first-passage time）。边界在采样前无从得知，但一个前缀是否已经越过边界，可以被精确判定。

1

Trajectory-specific轨迹特异

Each rollout has its own E, so the length decision has to be made per sequence.每条 rollout 都有自己的 E，所以长度决策必须逐条序列来做。

2

Exact first-passage精确首达

E is the hitting time of an integer counter. It is checked on the observed prefix and never estimated.E 是一个整数计数器的首达时间，直接在已观测的前缀上判定，从不估计。

3

Posterior后验性

E is unknown until y is sampled. So stopping has to move inside generation.在 y 被采样出来之前，E 是未知的，所以停止判断必须挪到生成过程内部。

STEP i步骤 i

Generate to k生成到 k

Resume from the cached prefix and decode only new positions. Each token is decoded once.从缓存的前缀继续，只解码新位置，每个 token 只解码一次。

STEP ii步骤 ii

Verify the count验证计数

Run the teacher and student on the new block and update the exact count ck. Locality and permanence keep earlier rewards unchanged.在新块上运行教师和学生，更新精确计数 ck。局部性与持久性保证之前的 reward 不会改变。

STEP iii步骤 iii

Stop or advance停止或推进

If c ≥ B, retire at the first crossing. Otherwise jump a fraction ρ of the estimated distance to the boundary.若 c ≥ B，在首次越界处停止；否则按估计的剩余距离向前跳 ρ 的比例。

c < B → advance k and repeatc < B → 推进 k，继续循环

The estimate decides when to look.估计只决定何时去看。

The trailing-window rate λ̂ only places the next checkpoint: big jumps far from the boundary, small steps near it. Under a stable rate this takes O(log B) rounds, and always at most 1 + ⌈(Lmax − k0)/kmin⌉.滑动窗口事件率 λ̂ 只用来放置下一个检查点：离边界远时大步跳，靠近时小步逼近。事件率平稳时只需 O(log B) 轮，最坏也不超过 1 + ⌈(Lmax − k0)/kmin⌉ 轮。

The count decides whether to stop.计数决定是否停止。

Proposition 1: E^≥E for any ρ ∈ (0, 1], any W, and any realization of λ̂. A bad estimate costs a few extra tokens (4.5%–10.9% overshoot in practice) and never any supervision.Proposition 1：对任意 ρ ∈ (0, 1]、任意 W 以及 λ̂ 的任意取值，都有 E^≥E。估计不准只会多解码几个 token（实测 overshoot 为 4.5%–10.9%），永远不会损失监督。

04 · Results04 · 主要结果

Fastest in every pair, without giving up accuracy.每组都最快，而且不牺牲准确率。

The setup trains on DAPO-Math-17K for 203 steps (64 prompts × 4 samples) on 8× H20 and evaluates mean@16 on five competition-math benchmarks with a 31,744-token budget. All runs use one fixed Flash-OPD configuration: η = 0.7, B = 200, ρ = 1.0, k0 = W = 128.在 DAPO-Math-17K 上训练 203 步（每步 64 个 prompt × 4 个样本），使用 8× H20；在五个竞赛数学 benchmark 上报告 mean@16，解码预算 31,744 token。所有实验使用同一组 Flash-OPD 配置：η = 0.7，B = 200，ρ = 1.0，k0 = W = 128。

07 · Limitations07 · 局限

What the guarantee does and doesn't cover这个保证覆盖什么、不覆盖什么

Compatibility proxy. Top-k overlap can be evaluated online on partial prefixes, but the stopping guarantee covers the boundary this criterion defines. It doesn't directly guarantee downstream accuracy. Richer signals that can still be computed online are an open direction.兼容性代理指标。Top-k overlap 可以在部分前缀上在线计算，但停止保证针对的是该准则所定义的边界，并不直接保证下游准确率。更丰富、且仍能在线计算的信号是一个开放方向。

Token-level only. Sequence-level objectives such as outcome rewards need the finished response, so applying this stopping rule directly could remove information the reward depends on. Future work targets multi-teacher OPD and agent tasks.仅适用于 token 级监督。outcome reward 这类序列级目标依赖完整回答，直接套用该停止规则可能丢掉 reward 所需的信息。后续工作将扩展到多教师 OPD 和 agent 任务。
